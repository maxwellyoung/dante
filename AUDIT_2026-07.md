## P0 — Foundations that were actively bleeding (two fixed today)

### 1. The build system could produce corrupted binaries silently — FIXED
The Makefile's object rules had **no header dependencies**. Change a struct in
`render.h`, rebuild incrementally, and every stale `.o` reads `GameCtx` at
wrong offsets — the game runs, renders, and passes QA while being subtly
wrong. This burned two hours today chasing a "shader bug" that was actually
ABI corruption, and it retroactively explains at least some past "why does
this look weird" sessions (the foggy corrupted lobby renders in QA history are
suspect). Fixed: `$(HDR)` prerequisite on all eight object flavors. Cost of
fix: one line. Cost of not having it: unbounded.

### 2. QA grades its own homework — RETIRE THE GRADES, KEEP THE MACHINE
`qa/grades.json` says **A, 96%** while you correctly say the graphics need a
lot of work. Both are true, which is the finding: the rubric measures
luma/contrast/hue-count heuristics — internal consistency, not the bar. The
bar is "does this frame hang next to Firewatch / Puzzle Agent." A heuristic
can't see that, so chasing the letter grade optimizes toward the rubric's
taste, and the March commits show exactly that drift ("harden color analysis,"
"QA heuristics" — six commits tuning the judge, not the picture).

**Keep** (genuinely production-grade, rare for a project this size):
screenshot-every-scene, baseline diffing, crash tests, headless tests, RALPH's
render→assess loop. **Retire**: letter grades as targets, PASS/FAIL on taste
metrics. **Replace with**: baseline diffs + side-by-side reference frames
(put an actual Puzzle Agent still next to the same-composition EV shot in
`qa/references/`) + your eye. The machine hands you comparisons; it stops
handing you verdicts.

### 3. Decide the flagship — RECOMMENDATION: SHIP EV SMALL, THEN AFTERSHOW
Per the narrative-arc notes, EV is ~97% built with one gap (the track). It is
15–30 minutes of finished-enough game. Shipping it — itch, free, credited solo
— converts eight months of engine work into a shipped-title reference of
intent for AFTERSHOW, exercises the full pipeline (playtest build exists), and
closes the loop the way Gravity Bone did for Blendo before Thirty Flights.
Killing it to pivot would throw away the one thing this repo hasn't produced
yet: a release. AFTERSHOW inherits everything either way.

## P1 — Graphics system: what it is, what it needs

### Honest assessment
**Strengths:** deep post-fx stack (ACES, CA, grain, dither, posterize, and now
luma cel bands — 10 presets); a lighting shader with 13 procedural materials,
PCF shadows, light pools; a real z-fighting discipline (decal system, tuned
clip planes); 1920×1200 as a design constraint; the shell system (Gehry
workflow) waiting to break the box.

**Weaknesses:** everything is `add_wall()` boxes, so composition has a
ceiling; 512px shadow map reads mushy at 1920×1200; one key + fill + 4 points
per scene means no authored light *rigs*; procedural material noise reads as
programmer wallpaper under the default look; no edge/outline pass; scene
geometry is hand-coded C, so spatial iteration is slow (the shell system was
built to fix exactly this and is still untested end-to-end).

### The Grickle result changes the strategy
Shift+0 now runs **luma-banded cel** (new shader path — quantizes brightness,
preserves hue; per-channel posterize hue-rotated into mud, so this needed the
shader, not just a preset row). The QA renders are the argument: under cel
bands + paper grain + inked contrast, the blockout geometry and the procedural
squiggle materials suddenly read as *deliberate illustration* — the wobble
lines look like pen strokes. The flaw-hiding you asked for is real.

**Direction:** stop treating style as a filter drawer and commit to one look
as the art direction (Grickle or a tuned variant for AFTERSHOW). Then:
1. **Tune lighting for banding** — fewer, stronger, more directional lights
   produce clean cel transitions; the current even fill fights the bands.
2. **Ink outline pass** — Sobel on depth+normals into the post chain. This is
   the single highest-value shader addition left; it completes the storybook.
3. **Shell system e2e test** — one organic room through Blender→GLB→collision
   to retire the box ceiling. (Gehry doc exists; never proven.)
4. Shadow map to 1024/2048 — cheap, visible.
5. The other nine styles: keep as shipped debug toys (they cost nothing), stop
   investing in them.

## P4 — Story structure: Bolaño, Godard (and Kaufman, the unnamed third)

*(Your message trailed "bolaño and godard and ," — I'm assuming Kaufman; he's
already in the canon docs. Correct me if the third was someone else.)*

**Bolaño gives AFTERSHOW its form.** *The Savage Detectives* is the shape of
this game: a polyphony of witnesses interviewed across years about a poet who
is never on the page. The forum IS the middle section of Savage Detectives —
usernames testifying around an absence. *2666*'s five parts orbit one crime at
different registers without ever entering it. Structural rules to steal:
- **The center stays absent.** The player never learns what happened between
  the hosts — not as a withheld twist, but because the investigation form
  outlives its object (the search becomes the story).
- **Evidence accumulates; resolution doesn't.** Each exhibit gets *more*
  ambiguous on inspection (the deleted tweet "works in every direction").
- **Parts, not acts** — discrete books at different registers/mediums: Twine
  prologue (the listener), 3D (the room), each self-contained, one absence.
- **Dignity-in-transit** (Amalfitano's geometry book on the washing line): the
  fan's spreadsheet of runtimes is his book on the line. Nobody comments.

**Godard gives it the grammar.** Not homage — techniques:
- **Chapter cards as structure** (Vivre sa vie's twelve tableaux): the FILE
  cards already in the prologue become the unifying device across both
  mediums — the 3D game keeps filing exhibits.
- **Jump cuts as honesty** (Breathless): the engine already has
  `hard_cut_to()`; Blendo carries this DNA. Cut *within* scenes, not just
  between — time skips mid-room when nothing worth watching happens.
- **Color as annotation** (Contempt's red): one accent color carrying meaning
  — RSS orange is AFTERSHOW's red; EV's French red stays EV's.
- **Sound/image divorce** (his essay films): podcast audio playing over the
  wrong spaces; narration contradicting what the room shows. The Green Room
  is built for this — audio evidence vs. rendered guess.

**Kaufman gives it the turn.** Unreliable interiority as architecture: the
Green Room re-renders under suspicion (already in the prologue's rules), and
the investigation was always about the investigator's own drifted friendships.
Synecdoche's warehouse-inside-warehouse is the Green Room's endgame if part
two wants a big swing: the fan builds the studio inside a space that is
itself inside his flat.
